About

I hold a BSc in Fisheries from Bangladesh Agricultural University, and I'm currently pursuing a Master's degree in Fisheries Management. My academic path has centered on understanding aquatic organisms not just as resources, but as living systems responding to environmental pressure.

My current interests sit at the intersection of eco-physiology — how organisms physiologically cope with environmental stress — climate change and its cascading effects on fish stocks and coastal communities, and the blue economy as a framework for sustainable, equitable use of aquatic resources.
